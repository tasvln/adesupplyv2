Built a **dynamic SVG card generator** for esports players and teams, turning live tournament stats into collectible cards similar to trading cards — used for recruitment, ownership, and fan collecting. Cards integrate directly with tournament databases so stats stay current in real time.

The system supports customizable card themes, interactive hover states, and easy exporting for both digital sharing and print.
