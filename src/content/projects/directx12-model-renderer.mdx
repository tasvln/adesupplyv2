Extended a custom DirectX 12 rendering framework with full **model rendering, advanced lighting, and a flexible material system**, integrating Assimp for asset importing across OBJ, FBX, and GLTF formats. Supports shadow mapping, normal mapping, and parallax mapping for real-time surface detail.

The renderer spans directional, point, and spot lights with Phong/Blinn-Phong shading, backed by a custom categorized logger and a real-time FPS counter.
